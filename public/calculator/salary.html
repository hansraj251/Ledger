Important: This is an estimated salary calculator. Actual salary depends on the employee's post, applicable allowances, posting, accommodation, NPS/UPS/other pension arrangements, recoveries, tax, CGHS and other department-specific deductions. Income tax/TDS is not automatically calculated here. HRA is calculated on Basic Pay. HRA rates automatically change according to the applicable DA slab: 24/16/8% up to 25% DA, 27/18/9% above 25% and up to 50% DA, and 30/20/10% above 50% DA for X/Y/Z cities respectively. Transport Allowance is calculated according to the selected Pay Level and posting category, with DA thereon.
