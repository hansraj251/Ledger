Service Delivery Policy

School Management Software

Last Updated: 2 September 2026

1. Digital Service

AlignOS School Management Software is a digital service. No physical product is normally shipped as part of a software subscription.

2. Access and Activation

After registration and, where applicable, successful payment, access is provided electronically through the supported website or application. Verification or onboarding steps may be required.

3. Service Scope

The service may provide Student Management, Staff Management, Classes, Attendance, Fee Management, Salary Management, Holidays, Dashboard information and authenticated school access, as described on the School Management page and applicable plan.

4. Updates and Maintenance

AlignOS may release updates, security improvements and feature changes. Temporary interruptions may occur during maintenance or because of circumstances outside reasonable control.

5. Customer Requirements

The school is responsible for suitable internet access, compatible devices, authorised user accounts and accurate information needed to use the service.

6. Support

For access or service issues, contact alignos.in@gmail.com and provide sufficient information to identify the school account and describe the issue.

7. Physical Shipping

Because the School Management Software is delivered digitally, shipping charges and physical delivery do not ordinarily apply.
